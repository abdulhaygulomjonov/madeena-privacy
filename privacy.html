Madeena — Maxfiylik siyosati

Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 12-noyabr

Qanday ma’lumot ishlatiladi

Madeena ilovasi Google akkauntingiz orqali kirishda faqat ikkita ruxsat so’raydi:

  • Email manzilingiz — qaysi akkaunt ulanganini ko’rsatish uchun.
  • Google Drive “appDataFolder” — ilovaning o’zi uchun yashirin papka. Ilova Drive’dagi boshqa fayllaringizni ko’rmaydi va o’zgartirmaydi.

Ma’lumot nima uchun kerak

Ilovadagi ma’lumotlaringiz (vazifalar, sanalar, sozlamalar va boshqalar) zaxira nusxasi sifatida shu yashirin papkada saqlanadi, shunda yangi telefonda shu akkaunt bilan kirib hammasini tiklashingiz mumkin.

Ma’lumot kimga beriladi

Hech kimga. Ma’lumotlaringiz bizning serverlarimizga yuborilmaydi, uchinchi tomonlarga sotilmaydi yoki berilmaydi, reklama uchun ishlatilmaydi. Hamma narsa faqat telefoningizda va o’zingizning Google Drive akkauntingizda turadi.

Ma’lumotni o’chirish

Ilovani o’chirib tashlasangiz, telefondagi ma’lumot o’chadi. Drive’dagi zaxira nusxani Google akkauntingiz sozlamalaridan (Google Account → Security → Third-party access → Madeena → Delete all connections) o’chirishingiz mumkin.

Madeena — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

What data is used

When you sign in with Google, Madeena requests only two permissions:

  • Your email address — to show which account is connected.
  • Google Drive “appDataFolder” — a hidden folder reserved for the app itself. The app cannot see or modify any other files in your Drive.

Why it is needed

Your app data (tasks, dates, settings, etc.) is stored as a backup in that hidden folder so you can restore everything on a new phone by signing in with the same account.

Sharing

None. Your data is not sent to our servers, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. It stays only on your phone and in your own Google Drive.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes the data on your phone. You can delete the Drive backup by revoking access in your Google Account (Security → Third-party access → Madeena → Delete all connections).
